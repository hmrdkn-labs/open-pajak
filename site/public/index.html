Rules that come from DJP’s own book

The PPh 21 and PPh 26 calculators cite Buku PPh 21/26, the guide from the Directorate General of Taxes (DJP) released on 8 January 2024. Each result comes with a step-by-step table and a formula narrative, so you can follow the rule instead of trusting a total.

The rule files sit in the repository next to the calculators, one per tax, written as plain functions.

Your numbers stay on your device

Open Pajak runs entirely in your browser, with no backend. It asks for numbers and transaction types, never for NIK, names or addresses.

Save a calculation as a receipt and it stays in your browser’s local storage. Export receipts to Excel or PDF, or upload a spreadsheet to create a batch of receipts offline.
